For brokers

What you need to know

Engage runs an assistant on WhatsApp that answers applicants about your listings, asks the right questions, updates the applicant record in Engage, confirms viewing commitments, and hands you a clear thread when it is time to act.

What happens on WhatsApp

  • The assistant replies quickly when an applicant messages about a property.
  • It confirms the property, keeps the natural tone, qualifies before you step in, and gets the viewing committed before you step in.
  • It does not negotiate, set final price, or commit on your behalf.

Rent or sale

Engage tells the assistant which journey to use from the listing. Rental: budget (annual rent), move-in date, cheque preference. Sale: budget (AED), payment terms, purpose. When all three for that journey are captured, the applicant is marked qualified in Engage.

Where applicants come from

In this rollout the assistant is for applicants who reach you from property portals where your stock is advertised.

Engage records

  • The assistant creates or updates the applicant as the chat moves.
  • The property stays linked; the record stays with the broker who owns the listing.
  • You see the WhatsApp context, notes, and stage (for example qualified or viewing).

Viewings

When the applicant is ready, the assistant captures the preferred viewing times and writes the request in Engage. You still confirm and own the appointment.

Spam and noise

Traffic is filtered in layers so empty, abusive, or obvious junk is dropped or closed before it crowds your queue.

Voice and audio

  • Applicants can send voice messages.
  • The assistant can handle voice and audio where enabled.

Testing and reporting

Test during normal work with real applicants. If something breaks, report it quickly with time, applicant phone, property reference, what happened, what you expected, and a screenshot if you have one. Send it to the group WhatsApp channel for this experiment.

How it works

How AI works alongside you

AI starts the chat, asks the key questions, and then hands over to you. Applicants can send voice messages; the assistant can handle voice and audio where enabled.

1. The applicant inquires

Someone sees one of your properties on the website, the portal, or a campaign and sends a WhatsApp message. AI receives it within a second.

2. AI replies in natural voice

It greets the applicant, confirms the property they are asking about, and starts a short, natural conversation. No forms, no callbacks.

3. AI asks the right questions, in order

For rentals: budget, move-in date, cheque preference. For sales: budget, payment terms, and why they are buying. Same standard every time.

4. The applicant is captured in Engage as it happens

AI creates the applicant record and the stage of the applicant the moment it has enough information, attaches the property, and updates the record turn by turn. The applicant record is assigned to you, the broker who owns the property, not to a shared inbox.

5. You receive a viewing-ready conversation

Once the applicant answers the key questions, the applicant moves to Qualified. You get the full context and take over.

What AI will not do

  • Close on your behalf. Negotiation, pricing decisions, and commitments stay with you.
  • Auto-book confirmed viewings end to end. Brokers receive an Engage notification with the appointment details, subject to your confirmation.
  • Replace your entire WhatsApp inbox. In this rollout it is focused on applicants who inquire from the portals.

Qualification

What AI qualifies, for each journey

Renters and buyers need different questions. AI knows which is which from the property the applicant inquired on, and adjusts.

Rental applicants

Budget
Annual rent the applicant is comfortable with.
Move-in date
When they need to be moved in by.
Cheques
Preferred number of cheques per year.

Once all three are captured, the applicant moves to Qualified.

Sales applicants

Budget
Total purchase budget in AED.
Payment terms
Cash, mortgage, or payment plan.
Purpose
End-use, investment, or both.

Same rule. All three captured, applicant moves to Qualified.

The handoff you receive

When a conversation reaches you, the applicant record already has:

  • The applicant's name, phone, and WhatsApp identity.
  • The property they inquired on, attached to the applicant record.
  • The qualified budget, terms, and timing.
  • Any preferences or context they shared during the conversation, captured as notes.
  • The conversation transcript, in case you want to read what happened before handoff.
You do not start the conversation from scratch. You continue it.

Viewings & continuity

Viewings

When an applicant is qualified and ready to view, AI captures the preferred slot and writes the request to the applicant record in Engage with full context.

Returning applicants are recognised

If an applicant has spoken to Engage before, AI recognises them. It does not create a duplicate record and it does not ask them their name again.

Spam and noise stay out

Two layers of protection sit in front of you:

  • Spam identification. Obvious junk and abusive patterns are filtered before they reach your queue.
  • Mid-conversation safety net. If a conversation turns out to be spam after a few turns, AI stops the conversation and marks the applicant as spam.

You should only see real applicants.

A walk-through example

10:42pm. Marina inquiry.

Sara sees a 2-bed property in Marina Heights on the portals and sends a WhatsApp message. AI replies in 1.4 seconds.

10:43pm. Conversation begins.

AI confirms she is asking about Marina Heights, then asks about budget, move-in date, and cheque preference. Sara is renting, AED 180k a year, moving in next month, 4 cheques.

10:46pm. Applicant record created in Engage.

The applicant record is attached to the Marina Heights property, assigned to the property's broker, marked Qualified. An AI-generated note captures Sara's preference for sea views.

10:47pm. Handoff.

AI tells Sara the broker will be in touch in the morning. The conversation is now yours.

8:15am. You arrive.

You open the applicant record, see the full picture, and call Sara. You can skip to the more detailed qualification and confirming a viewing.

Testing & reporting

Testing protocol

  • Test during normal daily work using real applicant conversations.
  • Do not use fake phone numbers or fake applicant data unless requested.
  • If something breaks, report it as soon as possible.

What to report

Share enough detail so the team can reproduce the issue quickly.

  • Time
  • Applicant phone
  • Property reference
  • What happened
  • What you believe should have happened
  • Screenshot (if available)

Where to report

Report issues to the group chat with the details on page 1.

Severity

  • Critical: work is blocked or applicants cannot continue.
  • High: major problem, but work can continue with a workaround.
  • Normal: small issue or cosmetic problem.
