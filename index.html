AI assistant, in your words.

AI is the AI assistant that picks up your WhatsApp applicants the moment they arrive, qualifies them with the right questions, writes everything into Engage, and hands you back a conversation that is ready to close.

You stop chasing names, budgets, and move-in dates. AI does that part on WhatsApp, the same way you would, and gives you back the time to do the work that actually moves a deal.

What changes for you

Every applicant gets a reply in seconds

AI answers the first WhatsApp message instantly, even at 11pm. You no longer lose applicants to the first broker who picks up.

You receive context, not phone numbers

By the time the conversation reaches you, the budget, move-in date, payment terms, and purpose are already on the applicant record.

Engage stays clean on its own

AI creates and updates the applicant record as the conversation moves. No copy-pasting from WhatsApp into Engage.

Spam and tyre-kickers stay out

A three-layer filter drops gibberish, rate-limit abuse, and obvious noise before it ever lands on your screen.

How it works

How AI works alongside you

AI starts the chat, asks the key questions, and then hands over to you.

1
Screenshot

Step-by-step strip: inquiry → reply → qualify → Engage → handoff

A single horizontal visual showing the five steps as small phone screens or numbered tiles. Used as the anchor image for this page.

1. The applicant inquires

Someone sees one of your properties on the website, the portal, or a campaign and sends a WhatsApp message. AI receives it within a second.

2. AI replies in your brand voice

It greets the applicant, confirms the property they are asking about, and starts a short, natural conversation. No forms, no callbacks.

3. AI asks the right questions, in order

For rentals: budget, move-in date, cheque preference. For sales: budget, payment terms, and why they are buying. Same standard every time.

4. The applicant is captured in Engage as it happens

AI creates the applicant record the moment it has enough information, attaches the property, and updates the record turn by turn. The applicant record is assigned to you, the broker who owns the property, not to a shared inbox.

5. You receive a viewing-ready conversation

Once the applicant answers the key questions, the applicant moves to Viewing. You get the full context and take over.

What AI will not do

  • Close on your behalf. Negotiation, pricing decisions, and commitments stay with you.
  • Auto-book confirmed viewings end to end. Brokers receive an Engage notification with the appointment details.
  • Handle all price ranges. In this experiment, AI only handles applicants up to AED 100,000 rent and AED 800,000 sale.

Qualification

What AI qualifies, for each journey

Renters and buyers need different questions. AI knows which is which from the property the applicant inquired on, and adjusts.

Rental applicants

Budget
Annual rent the applicant is comfortable with.
Move-in date
When they need to be in by.
Cheques
Preferred number of cheques per year.

Once all three are captured, the applicant moves to Qualified.

Sales applicants

Budget
Total purchase budget in AED.
Payment terms
Cash, mortgage, or payment plan.
Purpose
End-use, investment, or both.

Same rule. All three captured, applicant moves to Qualified.

The handoff you receive

When a conversation reaches you, the applicant record already has:

  • The applicant's name, phone, and WhatsApp identity.
  • The property they inquired on, attached to the applicant record.
  • The qualified budget, terms, and timing.
  • Any preferences or context they shared during the conversation, captured as notes.
  • The conversation transcript, in case you want to read what happened before handoff.
You do not start the conversation from scratch. You continue it.

Viewings & continuity

Viewings, with Engage broker notification

When an applicant is qualified and ready to view, AI captures the preferred slot and writes the request to the applicant record in Engage with full context.

The broker receives an Engage notification with the viewing details and a calendar invite link.

Property switching, no lost history

Applicants browse. A renter who inquired about Marina yesterday might send you a JBR property today. When that happens, AI handles the transition cleanly.

  • The previous property reference is recorded on the old applicant record before the conversation moves.
  • A consultant note is added so you can see exactly when and why the focus changed.
  • The new conversation starts fresh on the new property, but the applicant's profile and preferences carry over.

Returning applicants are recognised

If an applicant has spoken to Engage before, AI recognises them. It does not create a duplicate record and it does not ask them their name again. You see the existing history, not a new noise event.

Spam and noise stay out

Three layers of protection sit in front of you:

  • Rate limits and basic checks. Empty messages, oversized messages, gibberish, and known spam patterns are dropped automatically.
  • Classifier review. Borderline cases are reviewed by a fast classifier before reaching qualification.
  • Mid-conversation safety net. If a conversation turns out to be spam after a few turns, the session is closed quietly. Engage is marked so the customer record reflects the truth.

You only see real applicants.

Your day, in practice

What AI gives you every morning

By the time you open Engage, the overnight inquiries are already organised. Each applicant record carries:

  • The property the applicant came in on.
  • Budget, timing, and terms.
  • Stage. Most useful: who is in Viewing and ready for follow-up.
  • The full WhatsApp transcript and any notes AI captured along the way.

You decide the order of work. AI just makes sure the work is ready to be done.

What this means, day to day

  • Less time chasing details on WhatsApp. More time on the phone with applicants who matter.
  • No more lost applicants because nobody replied in time.
  • Engage that reflects reality, written by the conversation itself.
  • Higher viewing rates per applicant, because the easy ones are filtered in and the noise is filtered out.

Testing protocol

Keep testing simple and real.

  • Test during normal daily work using real applicant conversations.
  • Do not use fake phone numbers or fake applicant data unless requested.
  • If something breaks, report it as soon as possible.

What to report

Share enough detail so the team can reproduce the issue quickly.

  • Time
  • Applicant phone
  • Property reference
  • What happened
  • What should have happened
  • Screenshot (if available)

Where to report

Send the issue to the pilot support channel.

Severity

  • Critical: work is blocked or applicants cannot continue.
  • High: major problem, but work can continue with a workaround.
  • Normal: small issue or cosmetic problem.
